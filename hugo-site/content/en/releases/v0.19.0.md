Version **v0.19.0**, dated October 31, 2026, delivers a comprehensive concurrency, goroutine lifecycle, and shutdown resilience overhaul across the core routing engine, optional components, and the OpenTelemetry interceptor. Every component and observability module at v0.19.0 requires core v0.19.0.

## Core Concurrency & Lifecycle Hardening

- **`DirectConsumer`, `WireTap` & `lazyProducer` Shutdown Ordering**: Tracked active in-flight exchanges in `DirectConsumer` so `Stop()` waits for active deliveries to complete before stopping downstream processors and producers. Protected `WireTap` `WaitGroup` operations under a `sync.RWMutex` with a `stopped` flag and `CompleteExchange` completion, and prevented `lazyProducer` from recreating and leaking producers after `Route.Stop()`.
- **`Seda`, `Chan` & Concurrent `PollEnrich`**: Closed the race window between `Send()` and `Stop()` so exchanges enqueued concurrently with shutdown are drained rather than stranded. Replaced the boolean `polling` flag on `SedaEndpoint` and `ChanEndpoint` with a reference counter so concurrent `PollEnrich` callers can poll the same endpoint simultaneously.
- **Extended Deep Copying & $O(N)$ Parallel Split**: Extended `deepCopyValue` (`Exchange.Copy` / `Message.DeepCopy`) to clone `[]map[string]any` (SQL), `map[string]string`, `[]map[string]string`, `[][]string` (CSV), and `map[string][]byte` (Zip/TarGz). Added `Exchange.deepCopyWithoutInBody()` so parallel `Splitter` avoids $O(N^2)$ body cloning, and applied deep copying in `MemoryClaimCheckRepository` and `SagaState.clone()`.
- **Consumer & Management Lifecycle (`timer`, `net`, `http`, `management`)**: Added `stopping` lifecycle guards under mutex to prevent `Start()`/`Stop()` overlap during `WaitGroup.Wait()`, replaced one-shot `sync.Once` teardown in `HTTPConsumer` and `ManagementServer` to support clean restarts, closed TCP/UDP listeners via `context.AfterFunc` on context cancellation, and stopped `ManagementServer` first during `CamelContext.GracefulStop()`.
- **EIPs (`Aggregator`, `Resequencer`, `DeadLetterChannel`, `ContentEnricher`, `CircuitBreaker`)**: Prevented `Aggregator.timeoutWG` reuse races when `Stop()` runs without configured timeouts, tracked synchronous and asynchronous `Resequencer.deliver()` executions in `timeoutWG`, invoked `CompleteExchange` on timed-out, resequenced, and enriched exchanges, cleaned up `DeadLetterChannel.synchronized` entries on retry exhaustion or cancellation, and scoped `CircuitBreaker` state transitions to the state observed at call start.

## Component & Observability Improvements

- **`telegram`**: Guarded `TelegramConsumer.Start`/`Stop` with a mutex and per-start `sync.Once` on `StopReceivingUpdates()` (preventing double-`Stop()` panics), drained buffered updates on exit to prevent `tgbotapi` goroutine leaks, and rejected `TelegramProducer.Send()` after `Stop()`.
- **`mail`**: Released mutex locks before blocking IMAP/POP3 network I/O, honored `ctx` during connection establishment, enforced socket deadlines and forced connection close during `Stop()` to unblock stalled `idle.Wait()` / `Logout().Wait()` calls, and prevented `runWithIdle` from falling back to polling during shutdown.
- **`cron`**: Synchronized `CronComponent.scheduler` access across `Start()`/`Stop()`, waited for running jobs outside `CronComponent.mu`, and ensured `CronConsumer.Stop()` always cancels its startup context and waits on `startWG` even when `pauseJob=true`.
- **`kafka`**: Delayed `reader.Close()` in `KafkaConsumer.Stop()` until after `wg.Wait()` and committed in-flight processed offsets using a bounded `context.WithoutCancel(ctx)` timeout; added reference counting (`writerUsers`) to `KafkaEndpoint.writer` and closed the writer outside the endpoint mutex.
- **`redis`**: Verified `Ping(ctx)` in `RedisProducer.Start()` before publishing `p.connection`, and replaced one-shot `stopOnce` on `RedisConsumer` with mutex-protected `started`/`stopping` state.
- **`grpc`**: Bounded `GracefulStop()` with the endpoint timeout (falling back to `server.Stop()`), observed `ctx.Done()` in `GRPCConsumer.Start()`, and synchronized access to `server` and `listener`.
- **`file`, `ftp`, `sftp` & `smb`**: Cancelled the `WaitForStable` context on `FileConsumer.Stop()`, checked `ctx.Err()` across directory walks and file loops in `doPoll`, synchronized consumer `Start()`/`Stop()`, prevented `FTPProducer`/`SFTPProducer`/`SMBProducer` from dialing and leaking persistent connections after `Stop()`, and enforced a deadline on SMB handshakes.
- **`nats` & `stomp`**: Held the `stopping` state across `wg.Wait()` and `closeConn()` in `Stop()`, used `MustDisconnect()` in STOMP to avoid deadlocks on broken connections, bounded permanent STOMP ACK retry loops, and verified `p.started` in `Producer.Send()`.
- **`mongodb`, `ollama`, `openai`, `anthropic` & `jev`**: Synchronized producer lifecycle state under `sync.RWMutex`, returned an error instead of nil-pointer panicking when `MongoDBProducer.Send()` is called before `Start()`, and reset producer state on `Stop()`.
- **`observability/otel`**: Preserved `exchange.Context` when `next(exchange)` returns `ErrStopRouting`, preventing data races when exchanges are parked asynchronously by an `Aggregator`.
